Smooth circles, cones, curved shells and icospheres for Brickadia, saved straight to your Prefabs folder as .brz.

**Download:** `CircleGenerator.exe` below (or the .zip). No install needed – just run it. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the program isn't signed: choose *More info* → *Run anyway*.

### What's in it
- **◯ Circle** – full / half / quarter circles and ovals, solid, hollow, doughnut and inverse (round holes), in micro bricks or studs (Default, Tile, Smooth).
- **△ Cone** – sloped circles and ovals: cones, funnels, sloped doughnuts and cone-shaped holes.
- **◠ Shell** – domes, parabolic dishes, spheres and your own outlines (curve editor with drag points, sharp corners, snapping and a size scaler). Built from flat entity panels, optionally on working bearing hinges.
- **⬡ Icosphere** – geodesic spheres from 20 to 720 faces in micro wedges or Side Wedges, with a list of "best sizes" where the faces fit almost perfectly. Frozen, or held by bearings (the whole ball turns on one ground bearing).

Every prefab is self-checked before saving (no overlaps; joints land where they should).
